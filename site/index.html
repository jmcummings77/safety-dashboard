What these reports are. Florida's largest parks are exempt from state ride inspections because they employ their own inspectors. In return they must report any ride-related injury or illness that keeps a guest in hospital for 24 hours or more. These are the parks' own first reports, filed quarterly with FDACS.

What they are not. They are not maintenance or inspection records, and they do not establish cause. Many entries are medical episodes in guests with pre-existing conditions. FDACS receives no follow-up on a guest's condition. Counts depend on attendance and on how each park interprets the rule, so they don't rank ride safety.

Poor does not mean unsafe to cross: unsafe bridges are closed or load-posted.
